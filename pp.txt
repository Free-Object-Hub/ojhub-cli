PRIVACY POLICY, Object Hub (objecthub.xyz)
Version of 2026-10-09

1. WHO WE ARE
Object Hub (the "Site") is a non-commercial project for the Object Show community. It is run by an individual known as MIOBOMB (the "Operator"). Contact for anything related to your data: miobomb.real@gmail.com

2. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT
2.1. Account data: username, nickname, email address, password (stored only as a bcrypt hash), session token.
2.2. Device data for the devices you sign in from: User-Agent string, IP address, country and city (derived from the IP via GeoIP), and technical device fingerprints (static and dynamic). These are used to bind a device to an account and to prevent abuse.
2.3. Content you create: comments, likes, news, projects, vacancies, profile and portfolio (links, descriptions, resume, social links), poll votes, project subscriptions.
2.4. Support messages. They are stored only on the Operator's own servers.
2.5. Push notification subscriptions, if you enabled them.
2.6. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers on the Site. We do not build profiles for a personalized feed.
2.7. The fact that you agreed to the Terms and this Policy at registration: the date and the version of the documents you accepted.

3. COOKIES AND BROWSER STORAGE
We do not use cookies for analytics or tracking. The only cookie is a functional one that stores your chosen client version (cli_ver). Your session token and interface settings are stored in your browser so the Site can work.

4. HOW WE USE DATA
To run accounts and Site features, protect against spam and attacks, moderate content, send notifications, answer support requests, and let the administration audit actions.

5. THIRD PARTIES
5.1. Telegram. Some user actions (new comments, creating or editing projects and news, etc.) are logged to a private Telegram chat of the Operator for auditing and guaranteed delivery of notifications to the administration. Mostly only the fact of the action is recorded; the texts of comments and news are sent in full. IP addresses are not sent there. These records are kept indefinitely.
5.2. Browser push services (e.g. Google, Mozilla, Apple) deliver push notifications to your device if you enabled them.
5.3. CDN. For now the anti-bot widget (ALTCHA) may be loaded from the jsdelivr CDN, which can see your IP address.
5.4. A third-party email service may be added in the future; this policy will be updated.
5.5. We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers.
5.6. Contacts you choose to put in your profile, portfolio or vacancy applications are visible to other users and project authors. Share only what you are comfortable sharing.

6. WHERE DATA IS STORED
The Site's server is located in Iceland. The Operator is located in Russia. By using the Site you acknowledge that your data is processed in these countries.

7. RETENTION AND DELETION
7.1. Account data is kept as long as the account exists.
7.2. Full account deletion is not implemented yet. On request to miobomb.real@gmail.com we will anonymize your account: your email and password are erased and all linked devices are removed. Content you published (comments, news, projects) stays on the Site, no longer tied to your email.
7.3. Poll data: after a poll ends (at most 1 year), only the final aggregate results remain and the table of individual votes is deleted.
7.4. There is no data export feature yet.

8. SECURITY
Passwords are stored as hashes, and failed sign-ins are delayed to slow brute force. No system is perfectly secure; do not reuse on the Site a password you use elsewhere.

9. CHILDREN
9.1. The Site is not intended for people under 13. If you are under 13, do not use the Site.
9.2. If you are a parent or legal guardian and learn that a child under 13 has registered, write to miobomb.real@gmail.com and we will delete or anonymize the account. We will do the same if we learn about such an account ourselves.
9.3. If you are under 18, we recommend not putting personal contact details in your profile or vacancy applications and telling your parents about any collaboration through the Site.

10. CHANGES
This policy may change. The current version is always available on the Site; its date is shown at the top.

11. CONTACT
miobomb.real@gmail.com, or the "Write to support" form in your profile dashboard.
